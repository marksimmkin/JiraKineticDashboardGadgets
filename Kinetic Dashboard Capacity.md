A list of people with issues in the JQL, each with a bar comparing their remaining estimated work with the working days left in the filter's dates.

Treat a ticket's assignee as its user, with Unassigned as its own row last. Work is the sum of each person's Jira Remaining Estimate, in days of the hours per day set in this gadget's Edit view (default 8, the same for everyone). Issues without an estimate count as 0 and are counted per person. Working days are Monday to Friday from today, or the Start Date if later, to the End Date inclusive; holidays and leave are not counted. Allocation is work days divided by working days left: over 100% is red and labelled Overallocated, under 25% is blue and labelled Underallocated, otherwise green. Someone whose issues all lack an estimate gets an empty bar labelled No estimates instead. A line marks full capacity. Sort most allocated first. Unassigned work has no bar or label. The filter needs an End Date; once the dates have passed, or only weekend days are left, list each person's work without a judgement.

Show only statuses listed in the Controller with Show ticked, and show a "Hiding:" note naming the others. Hidden statuses and their issues are excluded from work, allocation and issue counts.
