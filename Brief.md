We want to develop a set of custom dashboard gadgets to use within our organisation, Kinetic Games.

These gadgets are intended to work together to give the team visibility on the progress of updates.

Where a gadget shows a count of issues, clicking that number opens Jira's standard issue list for the selected Controller JQL narrowed to the clicked status or assignee when applicable.
