A pie chart version of [[Kinetic Dashboard Status Bar Chart]].

Use the selected Controller JQL and its configured status names. Group same-name Jira statuses as in the bar chart. Show each selected status in the legend, including zero-count statuses; draw slices for nonzero counts. Percentages are of the issues in selected statuses, and show how many total JQL issues are outside that set.

Provide a status checkbox dropdown for the configured statuses. Hidden statuses have no slice or legend row and do not contribute to percentages or the selected-status count; the total JQL issue count remains available for comparison. Remember the selection for this gadget in the viewer's browser.
