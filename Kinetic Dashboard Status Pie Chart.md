A pie chart version of [[Kinetic Dashboard Status Bar Chart]].

Use the selected Controller JQL and its configured status names. Group same-name Jira statuses as in the bar chart. Show each shown status in the legend, including zero-count statuses; draw slices for nonzero counts. Percentages are of the issues in shown statuses, and show how many total JQL issues are outside that set.

Show only the statuses the Controller marks as shown, with a "Hiding:" note listing hidden ones. Hidden statuses have no slice or legend row and do not contribute to percentages or the shown-status count; the total JQL issue count remains available for comparison.
